Mechanics Guide

WASD to move, left click to Attack, P to pause, F to dodge, the mouse controls the camera. Attack and defeat enemies to obtain a buff and a drawback, and prepare to defeat the final boss of the level. Upon defeating the boss, you will be presented with the 'You Win' screen.



Objective

Explore the level and defeat enemies to obtain buffs and drawbacks, currently, defeating minions will give you increase movement and attack speed at the cost of increased damage. Your ultimate goal is to defeat the boss at the end of the level.



Pitch

Imagine a Fantasy 3D Action RPG where you were able to take the powers and drawbacks of enemies you defeat. Like having increased attack damage with slower movement when you defeat an Ogre. Instead of blindly killing everything in sight, you have to be careful and think about engaging in combat. With that mechanic and a multitude of enemies to face, a diverse weapon set and move set, each level can be approached strategically and you can build your character how you want to ultimately defeat the final boss of each level. You can also explore the region to find different treasures and weapons to help you in the level.



Synopsis

My game will be an 3D Action RPG where you control a character that can equip a weapon and engage in combat with various enemies, and defeating each enemy will give you a buff and debuff based on the enemy, which are only applied for that specific level. The few enemies I was thinking of would be; Goblins, who increase your attack speed at the cost of taking more damage, Ogres, who increase your attack damage at the cost of slower movement, and Knights, who increase your health at the cost of increasing enemy health as well. The player won’t know the effects of each enemy until they’re defeated, but a log will be provided to the player that updates for each enemy defeated. The enemies will also have a variety of weapons that they’ll drop, each having different attributes, mainly differing with attack speed and damage. There are also treasures to find to gain XP to improve your ‘permanent’ stats, including health, stamina, and strength. At the end of each level, there is a final boss that you have to defeat to finish, this is the primary objective of the game. Dying in the world will cause you to reset the entire level, that way you can approach the level differently and allow you to come up with a different strategy. For each level, you will start outside, and work your way towards a building where the boss is located. For the first level, you will spawn in an outside forest area and a castle at the end of the path. Currently, there is one weapon, one enemy type, and one boss. Currently, defeating minions increases your movement and attack speed at the cost of taking increased damage, so defeating all 9 minions would make you quite fast, but susceptible to being killed by the boss easier.
